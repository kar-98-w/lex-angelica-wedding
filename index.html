Filipino Wedding Traditions

These meaningful ceremony elements reflect the couple’s shared faith, unity, and hopes for married life.

◫

The Veil

The veil is placed over the couple as a symbol of unity and being clothed together in marriage.

∞

The Cord

The cord represents the lasting bond that joins the couple as one.

◉

The Coins

The wedding coins symbolize shared blessings, stewardship, and provision in married life.

✚

The Bible

The Bible represents the couple’s faith as a foundation for their marriage.
